THE GHOSTS

Blinky (Red): Relentless chaser targeting Pac-Man's tile directly.
Pinky (Pink): Strategic ambusher aiming 4 tiles ahead of Pac-Man.
Inky (Cyan): Flanker coordinating position with Blinky and Pac-Man.
Clyde (Orange): Wanders freely; approaches when far, flees when close.
